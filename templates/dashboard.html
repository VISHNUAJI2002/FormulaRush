THE MISSION

Why This Game Exists

Welcome to Formula Rush — a high-octane racing simulator where the engine isn't powered by fuel, but by your mind.

Every lane change, every burst of acceleration, every split-second dodge is triggered by solving rapid-fire math equations. Your cognitive speed IS your horsepower.

This isn't just a game — it's a neural training program disguised as a cockpit. The faster you think, the faster you drive. The sharper your mental arithmetic, the more obstacles you weave through.

🧠 CORE PHILOSOPHY

Traditional math drills are boring. Formula Rush solves this by coupling raw calculation with real-time consequences — miss a question, and you swerve into traffic. Nail it, and you accelerate through the field like a bullet. The dopamine of racing meets the discipline of arithmetic.

🔧 THE GARAGE

Your personal vehicle hangar. Cars are unlocked by progressing through Campaign levels and purchased with earned coins. Each tier looks and feels different on the track:

  • Bronze — Continental GT: Unlocked at Level 2. A solid all-rounder for pilots finding their groove.
  • Silver — 300 SL Roadster: Unlocked at Level 4. A sleek classic that rewards consistent performance.
  • Gold — Skyline GT-R: Unlocked at Level 6. The ultimate machine for pilots who've proven their mettle.

📊 PERFORMANCE ANALYTICS

Your telemetry dashboard logs every race. Track your reaction times, accuracy rates, and score progression over time with detailed graphs. Identify weak spots in specific math operations and watch your cognitive baseline improve session by session.

INPUT TECHNOLOGIES

Cyborg Interface Modes

Formula Rush supports three distinct input paradigms. In Multiplayer, each player can use a different input mode simultaneously — mix and match for maximum versatility.

⌨️ KEYBOARD — STANDARD PROTOCOL

The default cockpit input. Type your answer using either the number row or the numpad, then press Enter to lock it in. In multiplayer, Player 1 uses the left-side keys and Player 2 uses the right-side numpad — no interference.

🎙️ VOICE CONTROL — VOCAL OVERRIDE

Speak your answers directly into your microphone. The system uses the Web Speech API for real-time recognition. Say the number clearly and it locks in automatically — no hands needed. For combat mode, shout "Fire", "Shoot", or "Attack" to launch an EMP, and "Shield", "Defend", or "Block" to activate your barrier.

🖐️ GESTURE CONTROL — NEURAL LINK

Use your webcam to answer by holding up fingers. The system counts the number of visible fingers in real-time using hand-tracking AI. For combat: make a fist (0 fingers, 2 hand) to Fire EMP, or show open palms (10 fingers, 2 hands) to activate Shield. The ultimate hands-free experience.

GAME MODES

Choose Your Racing Protocol

🏎️ QUICK RACE — SOLO PRACTICE

Endless survival mode. Configure your session from the Mission Config screen:

  • Gear Shifter: Select difficulty — Easy (single-digit), Medium (double-digit), or Hard (multi-operation chains).
  • Math Loadout: Choose specific operations — multiplication tables, squares, cubes, square-roots, or a full random mix.
  • Power-ups: Purchase a Hull Shield (blocks 1 hit), Nitro Boost (fast start), or AI Co-Pilot before launch.

Dodge oncoming traffic by answering correctly. Wrong answer = wrong lane = collision. Survive as long as you can.

🏆 CAMPAIGN — RANKED PROGRESSION

A structured series of missions with escalating difficulty. Each level focuses on a specific math operation and must be completed to unlock the next. Progress through the campaign to earn coins, unlock new car tiers, and climb the ranks from Rookie → Amateur → Pro → Legend.

⚔️ MULTIPLAYER — TACTICAL MODE

1v1 split-screen racing with a built-in Tactical Combat System. Both players race side-by-side, answering questions to steer. Set the race duration (60s / 90s / 120s / custom) and choose individual input modes per player.

⚡ COMBAT SYSTEM BREAKDOWN

  • 🔥 Ammo Charge: Get a streak of 5 correct answers in a row to earn one Ammo charge.
  • 💥 FIRE EMP: Spend your Ammo to launch an electromagnetic pulse at your opponent. A direct hit slows their car significantly for several seconds.
  • 🛡️ ACTIVATE SHIELD: Alternatively, spend your Ammo to raise a protective barrier. If your opponent fires an EMP while your shield is active, the attack is completely blocked.
  • 🎯 Strategy: The decision to fire or shield is the core tactical layer. Do you strike aggressively, or play defensive when you suspect incoming fire? Timing is everything.
